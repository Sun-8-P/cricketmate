Frequently Asked Questions

My match data disappeared. What happened?

All match data is stored locally on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app will erase your match history. We recommend taking a screenshot of important scorecards before uninstalling.

Can I edit a match after it has been completed?

You can undo deliveries ball by ball during a match. Once a match is marked complete, individual balls cannot be edited, but you can view the full scorecard at any time.

How do I adjust overs during a rain delay?

During a live match, tap the pencil icon next to the overs display in the scoring screen. You can increase or decrease the total overs from there.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription at any time through your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → CricketMate.

How does the free trial work?

CricketMate offers a free trial with full access. You will not be charged until the trial period ends. You can cancel anytime before the trial ends to avoid being charged.

I was charged after cancelling. What do I do?

All billing is handled by Apple. Please contact Apple Support at support.apple.com for billing issues.
