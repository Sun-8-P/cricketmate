Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

CricketMate is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

All match data and app content is stored locally on your device. We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information on our servers.

We use the following third-party services which may collect limited data:

  • Google AdMob — serves ads within the app. May collect an anonymous device identifier (IDFA) for ad personalisation. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription state. May collect anonymised purchase data. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Tracking

CricketMate uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. You will be asked for permission before any advertising identifier is used for cross-app tracking. You can change this permission at any time in your iPhone Settings.

Data Storage

All user data and match history is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Children's Privacy

CricketMate is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
